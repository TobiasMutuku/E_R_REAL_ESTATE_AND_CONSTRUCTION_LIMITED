Privacy information

This page explains how information submitted through this website is currently handled. The company should review and approve this notice, including its retention periods and applicable legal details, before production use.

Information you provide

Contact enquiries may include your name, email address, phone number, enquiry type, selected property, subject, and message. Quote requests may include your name, email address, phone number, project type, project location, estimated budget, preferred timeline, project brief, and an optional plan or site-photo attachment.

How it is used

Enquiry information is stored in the website database so authorized administrators can review and respond to requests. Quote attachments are stored with the associated quote request. Submitting a form does not guarantee a quotation, service, or response time.

Website operations

The forms use a session token to help prevent forged submissions and a hashed network identifier for rate limiting; stale rate-limit records are periodically cleaned up. Web-server logs and essential session data may also be generated as part of operating the site.

Access, retention, and questions

Access to stored enquiries is intended for authorized administrators. The company must set and communicate a retention schedule and process for access, correction, or deletion requests before production. To ask about information submitted through this site, contact errealestateconstruction@gmail.com.
